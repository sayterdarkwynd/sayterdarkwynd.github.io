Level Designer

  • • Worked collaboratively with team members to design and implement 100+ levels.
  • • Worked with the project manager to design character abilities and features.
  • • Created art assets for biome textures and abilities, and composing them into tilemaps for use in 2d level designs.
  • • Prototyped and tested gameplay features and mechanics.

As levels in Soulreaper were composed entirely of tiles, my planning and blockouts were created the same way, though without wasting any time with high-fidelity files. There simply wasn't time, or manpower. I kept things simple - each biome was set up as a singular progression 'chart' image. Areas were linked via lines, and necessary level features were placed down as icons from a legend. These mockups were then translated directly into the tile system to match the exact design.
